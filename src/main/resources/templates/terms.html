Terms

Rules for using portfinder as a discovery catalog.

What this site is

portfinder indexes metadata and outbound links to game decompilation and recompilation projects. It is a discovery catalog, not a ROM or game-file host.

Allowed content

  • Project names, summaries, status, platforms, tags, and links to official repos or homepages
  • Optional preview images hosted by the project or other legitimate public sources

Prohibited

  • Uploading or linking to ROMs, ISOs, dumps, or other copyrighted game assets for download
  • Misleading submissions, spam, or abuse of voting and submit flows

Accounts and moderation

Voting and submitting require Google sign-in. Moderators may approve, reject, or remove submissions that violate these terms or applicable law. We may rate-limit votes and submissions to protect the service.

Third-party projects

Linked projects keep their own licenses and policies. portfinder does not claim ownership of those projects and is not responsible for their content or builds.

Availability

The service is provided as-is. Free-tier hosting may sleep or change without notice.
